Melbourne TripAgent

A Chinese-language travel planner for 1–3 days in Melbourne. The model only extracts the request; a deterministic planner builds the itinerary from 40 sourced POIs, with budget, replacement and evidence checks. Includes a 30-task fixed evaluation set.
